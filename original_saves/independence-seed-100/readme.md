These saves are DOS every-turn autosaves of a Dutch player. VR_SEED.EXE is used to fix random seed at 100.

Whenever a popup happens, the player dismisses it or takes the first option. Similarly, unit moves are skipped. (Both can be accomplished in DOS by pressing SPACEBAR.) 

Between save 2 and 3, player declares independence (this requires clicking the GAME menu, selecting DECLARE INDEPENDENCE from the dropdown and then selecting the second option on the resulting popup). Thereafter, the player continues to idleness, dismissing popups, choosing first options and passing unit turns and ending turn.
