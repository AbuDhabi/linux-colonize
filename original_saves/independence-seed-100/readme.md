These saves are DOS every-turn autosaves of a Dutch player. VR_SOUND.EXE is used to fix random seed at 100 and disable music burning RNG invocations.

Whenever a popup happens, the player dismisses it or takes the first option. Similarly, unit moves are skipped. (Both can be accomplished in DOS by pressing SPACEBAR.) 

Player declares independence immediately after loading the first save and receiving control (this requires clicking the GAME menu, selecting DECLARE INDEPENDENCE from the dropdown and then selecting the second option on the resulting popup). Thereafter, the player continues to idleness, dismissing popups, choosing first options and passing unit turns and ending turn. The player simulated this by continually pressing the spacebar.
