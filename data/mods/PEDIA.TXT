@MODBUILDING10
@width=300
^{TOWN HALL EXPANSION}
As a colony grows, its town hall grows with it. The expanded hall gives
the colonial government reach over more of the surrounding land. It can
be built when your population is 4 and the colony has a town hall. {The
colony can work 12 plots: the 8 around it plus the 4 plots two squares
away to the north, south, east and west.}

@;
@MODBUILDING11
@width=300
^{COLONIAL ASSEMBLY}
A colonial assembly is an elected body that governs the colony and the
country around it. It can be built when your population is 8 and the
colony has a town hall expansion. {The colony can work 20 plots: every
plot within two squares except the four corners.}

@MODMISC12
@width=300
^{RESTORED CUT CONTENT}
Features the original game planned but left unfinished. Choose them on
the title menu (MODS for New Games); each new game keeps its own choice.
^{King's Audience}: ask the King for an audience from the VIEW menu.
^{Royal Mercy}: a lost Royal unit lowers the tax.
^{Town Hall Expansion, Assembly}: larger colonies work more land.
^{Tribe Mood}: chiefs tell you what angers their tribe.
^{Treaty Cancel Notice}: your advisor reports a cancelled treaty.
^{Loot Wagon Trains}: natives may seize a beaten wagon's goods.
^{Raid Scalps}: a native raid may kill a colonist.
^{Scout Espionage}: rival scouts spy on your colonies.

@;
@MODMISC13
@width=300
^{NEW RULES AND FEATURES}
Changes to the rules and new orders. Choose them on the title menu (MODS
for New Games); each new game keeps its own choice.
^{Abandon Colonies}: buildings need a minimum population, and a colony
^can be abandoned.
^{Late Founding Fathers}: five Fathers wait until 1650, Smith until 1600.
^{Building Upkeep}: buildings cost gold each turn, or work at half.
^{Hard King}: a tougher Royal Expeditionary Force.
^{Plan Pioneer}: plan a pioneer's work, then let it automate.
^{Scouts and Ships Auto-Explore}: they explore on their own.
^{Current Vision}: land no unit or colony sees now is veiled.

@;
@MODMISC14
@width=300
^{DOS BUG FIXES}
Fixes for mistakes in the original game. They are on by default; switch
one off on the title menu (MODS for New Games) to play as the original.
^{Go To Waits at Natives}: a unit sent to a tile held by a native unit
^waits next to it instead of attacking.
