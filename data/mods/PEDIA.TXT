^{TOWN HALL EXPANSION}
As a colony grows, its town hall grows with it. The expanded hall gives
the colonial government reach over more of the surrounding land. It can
be built when your population is 4 and the colony has a town hall. {The
colony can work 12 plots: the 8 around it plus the 4 plots two squares
away to the north, south, east and west.}

@MODBUILDING11
@width=300
^{COLONIAL ASSEMBLY}
A colonial assembly is an elected body that governs the colony and the
country around it. It can be built when your population is 8 and the
colony has a town hall expansion. {The colony can work 20 plots: every
plot within two squares except the four corners.}

@MODMISC12

^{RESTORED CUT CONTENT}
Features the original game planned but left unfinished. Choose them on the
title menu (MODS for New Games); each new game keeps its own choice.
^{King's Audience}: once a turn, VIEW King's Audience (K) lets you ask the
King for his blessing, a specialist or a grant of gold (both raise your tax),
lower taxes, or independence.
^{Royal Mercy}: when you lose a ship or artillery, the King lowers your tax
by that unit's own amount and tells you so.
^{Town Hall Expansion, Assembly}: new buildings. The expansion (population 4)
works 12 plots, the assembly (population 8) 20, up to two squares away.
^{Tribe Mood}: a chief tells your scout how his people feel and what angers
them most: roads, cleared forest, missions, crowding or attacks.
^{Treaty Cancel Notice}: when a rival nation cancels its peace treaty with you,
your foreign affairs advisor tells you, as for a declaration of war.
^{Loot Wagon Trains}: natives who beat a loaded wagon train may take one kind
of goods, or all of them, instead of destroying it. The wagon survives.
^{Raid Scalps}: a native raid may kill a colonist or an unarmed unit in the
colony. A fort protects those inside; a fortress stops it.
^{Scout Espionage}: rival nations send scouts into your colonies. A caught
spy is lost; one that gets in reports the colony's defence, and a weak colony
may be attacked.

@MODMISC13
@width=300
^{NEW RULES AND FEATURES}
Changes to the rules and new orders. Choose them on the title menu (MODS for
New Games); each new game keeps its own choice.
^{Abandon Colonies}: every building needs its minimum population, so colonists
cannot leave below it. An ABANDON button on the colony screen gives the colony
up; its colonists walk out.
^{Late Founding Fathers}: Stuyvesant, Washington, Jones, Jefferson and Franklin
cannot join Congress before 1650, Adam Smith not before 1600, for any nation.
^{Building Upkeep}: each turn your colonies pay gold for their buildings. If the
treasury cannot pay, buildings work at half and teachers do not teach that
turn. The colony report shows the total.
^{Hard King}: the Royal Expeditionary Force lands more troops each turn, seizes
occupied tiles, and aims at colonies with few soldiers nearby.
^{Plan Pioneer}: VIEW Plan Pioneer marks roads, plowing and forest clearing on
the map; ORDERS Automate sends the pioneer to do the work.
^{Scouts and Ships Auto-Explore}: ORDERS Automate (Q) sends a scout to villages
and rumours, or a ship to uncharted sea, on its own.
^{Current Vision}: land you have explored but no unit or colony of yours sees
now is shown veiled.

@MODMISC14
@width=300
^{DOS BUG FIXES}
Fixes for mistakes in the original game. They are on by default; switch
one off on the title menu (MODS for New Games) to play as the original.
^{Go To Waits at Natives}: in the original, a unit sent by Go To to a tile where
a native unit stands attacks it. With the fix it stops next to the tile and
waits, keeping its order, and goes on once the tile is free.
